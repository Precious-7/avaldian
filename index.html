Questions we get asked.

How much do I need to spend on ads to start?

Enough for the platform to learn from, which in practice means a budget that produces a handful of conversions a week. We work out that number for your service before you commit, and if it's too small to be worth running, we'll tell you.

How long before I see results?

Search ads often bring enquiries in the first week, because those people were already looking. Social usually takes two to four weeks, since the audience wasn't searching for you. A two-week pilot shows you the direction, not the final numbers.

Do I need a new website first?

Not always. If your current page loads quickly on a phone and has one obvious way to contact you, we'll run ads straight to it. If it doesn't, the ads will burn money, and we'd rather say so than take the work.

Who owns the ad account and the data?

You do. Use the account you already have, or we open one in your name and you grant us access. You can revoke it at any point and everything stays with you.

What is answer engine optimisation?

People increasingly ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI summaries for a recommendation instead of scrolling a results page. Those systems quote pages that answer a question plainly. We structure your site so it gets quoted, rather than only chasing a ranking.

What if the ads don't work?

You stop. The pilot is deliberately small so that finding out costs you very little, and you'll have the numbers to see it for yourself rather than taking our word for it.
